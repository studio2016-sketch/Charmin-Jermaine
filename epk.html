THE ARTISTS

A refined sound for a world-class destination.

Charmin & Jermaine blend expressive saxophone, bass-centered artistry, sophisticated arrangements and the warmth of R&B into a flexible live experience for premium hospitality environments. Their approach is musical rather than background-only: elegant enough for sunset cocktails and fine dining, but dynamic enough for a featured evening set, gala or beach-stage celebration.

Saxophonist · Featured Artist · Horn Arranger

Charmin Greene

Charmin brings a soulful, vocal-like approach to saxophone with a strong sense of melody, atmosphere and connection. Her sound moves comfortably through R&B, jazz, soul, pop and gospel-influenced music.

charmingreene.com →
Bassist · Multi-Instrumentalist · Musical Director

Jermaine Williams

Jermaine anchors the duo with bass-centered artistry, musical direction and broad multi-instrumental experience. His work is shaped by deep pocket, harmony, production awareness and a concert-minded sense of pacing.

jermainewilliams.com →
WHY IT WORKS

Premium hospitality without losing the concert.

  • Luxury-resort appropriate visual presentation and premium musical vocabulary.
  • Flexible energy for sunset lounge, cocktail hour, dinner, private VIP event, gala and featured-stage programming.
  • International repertoire spanning familiar R&B, soul, pop and jazz-influenced selections alongside original music.
  • Compact signature duo footprint with professionally produced support tracks and the ability to scale upward.
  • Warm, polished guest interaction that supports the environment rather than overwhelming it.

MUSICAL DIRECTION

Familiar feeling. Elevated expression.

The core sound is instrumental R&B with jazz fluency and soul. Programming moves seamlessly between relaxed ambient sets and more rhythmic, recognizable material. Sets can be curated around guest demographic, resort concept, meal period, special event or festive-season brief.

PRODUCTION & LOGISTICS

Professional, scalable and easy to advance.

Signature duo — core needs

  • Professional stereo PA appropriate to venue
  • 2 artist monitor/IEM mixes as available
  • Bass DI input
  • Saxophone wireless or quality clip-on/instrument microphone
  • Stereo playback inputs for support tracks
  • 2 vocal microphones for announcements / optional vocals
  • Standard AC power and safe performance area

International engagement

  • Based in Houston, Texas, USA
  • Available for international travel, subject to contract and entry requirements
  • Backline / instrument transport coordinated during advance
  • Hospitality, lodging, ground transport, flights and work authorization defined in contract
  • Expanded production rider supplied for full-band or orchestral formats
