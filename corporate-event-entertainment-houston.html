BOOKING QUESTIONS

What kinds of Houston corporate events are a fit?

Corporate galas, executive receptions, awards programs, client events, fundraising events, premium hospitality and selected brand experiences can all be considered.

Yes. The approach is designed to support the room while retaining an identifiable artist-led sound and the option for a featured performance set.

Can the ensemble work with an event producer or AV team?

Yes. Audio, stage, playback, backline, timing and other production requirements are advanced with the event team.

Yes. Options range from the signature duo to full band, R&B orchestra, jazz orchestra and custom concert-scale arrangements.
