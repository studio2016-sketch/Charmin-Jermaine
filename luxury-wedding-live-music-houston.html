BOOKING QUESTIONS

What planners and hosts usually want to know.

Can Charmin & Jermaine perform at a Houston wedding reception?

Yes. Wedding inquiries can be built around the signature duo, full band, R&B orchestra or jazz-orchestra format depending on the venue, guest count and desired energy.

Can the music work for cocktail hour and dinner as well as a featured set?

Yes. Programming can move from refined instrumental sets into more rhythmic and recognizable material, with the performance format scaled to the event.

Do you work with wedding planners and venue production teams?

Yes. Production, timing, backline, stage, travel and other advance details are coordinated for each engagement.

Are destination weddings available?

Yes. Charmin & Jermaine are Houston-based and available for destination and international engagements subject to contract, travel and entry requirements.
