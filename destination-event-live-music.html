BOOKING QUESTIONS

What planners and hosts usually want to know.

Are Charmin & Jermaine available internationally?

Yes. International engagements can be considered subject to contract, entry requirements, travel logistics, production and scheduling.

What travel items are typically advanced?

Flights, lodging, ground transport, hospitality, backline or instrument transport, production requirements and work authorization are defined as needed for the engagement.

Can the duo travel with a compact footprint?

Yes. The signature duo is designed as a compact artist-led format supported by professionally produced playback, with the ability to scale upward.

Can you provide a larger ensemble for an international event?

Yes. Full-band, R&B orchestra and jazz-orchestra options can be developed according to the event, venue and production plan.
