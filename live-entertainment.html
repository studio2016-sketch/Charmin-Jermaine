DIRECT ANSWERS

Common booking questions.

What performance formats do Charmin & Jermaine offer?

Performance formats include the signature artist duo, full band, R&B orchestra, jazz orchestra and custom concert-scale presentations. The right format depends on the room, audience, program and desired experience.

Can Charmin & Jermaine perform at luxury weddings and private events?

Yes. The duo and expanded ensembles can be considered for luxury weddings, private celebrations, corporate galas, hospitality events and destination engagements.

Can the ensemble scale for concerts and festivals?

Yes. The presentation can scale from duo to full band and larger horn, string and orchestral formats according to venue size, audience expectations and production requirements.

How are production requirements handled?

Audio, backline, stage, rehearsal, travel and production requirements are advanced for each engagement so the musical format integrates cleanly with the event.
